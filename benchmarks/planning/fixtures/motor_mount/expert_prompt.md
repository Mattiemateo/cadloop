# Synthetic removable motor mount

Make a compact removable motor mount for the explicitly supplied motor and
extrusion. These are synthetic fixture dimensions; do not infer a motor family
or import an external specification.

The motor envelope is square, 42 mm on each side. Its four M3 mounting positions
form a centered square with a 31 mm pitch. Provide a centered shaft clearance
with a 24 mm diameter. The extrusion cross-section is square, 15 mm on each side.
Orient the shaft outward, away from the extrusion, on a shared motor axis and
shaft-clearance axis. Keep the motor removable with tool access.

Print the fabricated mount in PETG. Prefer an orientation that needs no supports;
this is a manufacturing preference, not proof that any geometry will print well.
Minimize the envelope while retaining the fixed mating dimensions and assembly
access. Wall thickness, cosmetic fillets and the clamp implementation remain
explicit CAD design choices; do not invent structural approval.

Keep geometry parameters editable, preserve the supplied dimensions, and use
independent geometric verification after CAD generation. No load, torque,
extrusion slot profile, motor depth, thread engagement or strength rating has
been supplied. Any such requirement needs separate measured evidence or user
review before engineering approval.
