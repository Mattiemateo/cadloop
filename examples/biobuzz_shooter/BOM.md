## Main shaft axial stack

Assembly coordinates are Y along the shaft. Main shaft ends: Y=−57 and +83 mm. Wheel core hub spans −22..+22 mm. Wheel-hub collars occupy −30..−22 and +22..+30 mm. Nominal shaft-retaining collars sit against the inner bearing faces at −47.05..−39.05 and +39.05..+47.05 mm. They pass inside the plywood/carrier bores; confirm contact is on the bearing's **inner ring only**, with no seal or stationary-carrier rubbing. Do not use the printed reference annulus as evidence of actual inner-ring width.

Right main-shaft sprocket envelope: Y=58.55..73.55 mm; its outer collar: Y=73.55..81.55 mm. The motor sprocket and outer collar use the same axial planes. The driver shaft is nominally Y=38..83 mm; **verify output socket engagement and set-screw contact before cutting stock**. Align actual tooth planes rather than assuming the two sides of each asymmetric hub are interchangeable. Shaft sleeve fits, collar placement and free rotation need a physical assembly check.

The 34-pitch chain calculation includes the full closed loop. Do not add a master link in a way that increases the prescribed pitch count. Chain thickness, master-link envelope, tension, pulley rotation and all fastener clearances must be checked under the fitted guard; CAD uses simplified sprocket envelopes.
